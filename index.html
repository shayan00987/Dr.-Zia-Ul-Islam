BEFORE YOU VISIT

Questions worth asking.

Every procedure has individual considerations. Use your consultation to discuss your health, goals, alternatives, risks, costs, and recovery.

Book a consultation ↗
How do I know which procedure is right for me?+

A consultation allows the surgeon to understand your goals, assess your individual circumstances, and explain suitable options and alternatives. No procedure is appropriate for everyone.

Will my results look natural?+

Outcomes vary with anatomy, healing, technique, and individual goals. Discuss what is realistic for you and review relevant examples with the clinician during your consultation.

How long is recovery?+

Recovery depends on the procedure and your health. Your surgeon should explain expected downtime, aftercare, warning signs, and when you may return to normal activities.

Can I book online?+

Yes. Use the appointment link on this website to open the supplied scheduling page and check available appointment options.
