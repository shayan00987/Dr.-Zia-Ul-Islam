Surgeon Introduction

Surgery guided by restraint, not trend.

Dr Zia Ul Islam is a Consultant Plastic & Aesthetic Surgeon, qualified FRCS (Plast) and board certified in both Pakistan and the United Kingdom. His practice is built around a simple belief: the best result is the one that looks like you, only refreshed.

Every plan begins with a careful conversation and is shaped around the individual, never a template. The aim is natural, balanced, lasting outcomes with honest guidance from consultation to recovery.

  • NaturalResults that respect your own features.
  • RefinedMeticulous technique and quiet detail.
  • IndividualizedA plan designed around you.
